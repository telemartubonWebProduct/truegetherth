# Hero picture: sources and prompts

**Since 9 Oct 2026 the home page opens with a layered oil painting instead: see `tableau/README.md`.** What follows is the first hero, a 3D router and a robot arm, kept as the record of how it was made. Its files are no longer on the site (`public/models`, `public/media/hero-v3` and the scripts `hero-arm.mjs` and `hero-poster.mjs` were removed; `scripts/blender/router.py` still builds the model). `router/poster-v3.webp` is its still: the share picture (`scripts/brand/logo.mjs`) and the router reference for the painting (`tableau/reference/router-plain.png`) come from it.

## What the site uses now

- `router/reference-true-wifi7.webp` — the photo of the True "Smart Home Router" (Wi-Fi 7) the owner gave as the reference for the 3D model (`scripts/blender/router.py`). `router/trueonline.png` is the True Online mark from the owner's own site, used as the logo on the model.
- `parts/` — the robot arm on green, generated by the owner with Nano Banana Pro at 2K on 3 Oct 2026, from the arm prompts below. The generator's file names are misleading: `Robot_arm_pointing_index_finger…jpg` is the hand **open**, `Studio_product_photograph_of_a…jpg` is the hand **pointing**. `Wi-Fi_router_product_photograph…jpg` is a generated generic router, not used (the model replaced it). `scripts/hero-arm.mjs` cuts the two arms out.

## Earlier attempts, kept for reference (not used by the site)

- `reach-v1-seedream.png` — a generated photo of a hand and a generic router (2816×1584). Higgsfield, model Seedream 5.0 Flash, 2K, 16:9, 3 Oct 2026, 0.5 credit. The site first used it cut into two layers.
- `flow/frame-1-start.png`, `flow/frame-2-end.png` — first and last frame of a reach made from that photo, for a video generator that animates between two frames (the video prompts below). The owner chose the 3D model and cut-out arm instead of a video.

Higgsfield notes (free plan, checked 3 Oct 2026): GPT Image 2.5 and Seedream 4.5 answer "Requires basic plan or higher"; Seedream 5.0 Flash works. Video starts at 2 credits (480p, 4 s) and a sharp 1080p clip is about 10–12, so a video needs a top-up or another tool.

## Arm prompts (Nano Banana Pro, 16:9, 2K, on chroma green; cut out by `scripts/hero-arm.mjs`)

Do not ask the generator for a transparent background: it paints a checkerboard. Flat green is keyed out, and since the arm has no colour of its own it is saved in grey, which removes any green cast.

### Pointing

```text
Studio product photograph of a single sleek humanoid robot arm, isolated on a perfectly flat, uniform, pure chroma-key green background (#00FF00).

Straight side-on view at eye level. The arm is perfectly horizontal: it enters from the left edge of the frame (the forearm is cut off by the left edge) and extends to the right, with the hand on the right side of the frame. The hand points to the right with the index finger fully extended and straight, the other three fingers curled loosely downward, thumb relaxed. The fingertip stops at about 90 percent of the frame width, leaving a small margin of green on the right. The arm is vertically centred with clear green above and below.

Glossy white ceramic shell panels over black graphite mechanical joints with polished chrome pins, a black segmented wrist joint between forearm and hand, intricate realistic detail. The robot itself is strictly monochrome: white, grey, black and chrome only.

Soft, even, diffused studio light from directly above and slightly in front, neutral white light, no green reflections and no green tint on the robot. No floor, no surface, no shadow on the background, no gradient or vignette in the background.

Entire arm in razor-sharp focus, no depth-of-field blur, no motion blur, 85mm lens, ultra detailed. No text, no logos, no other objects, no people.
```

### The same arm, hand open (attach the pointing picture as the reference)

```text
Use the attached image as the exact reference. Keep everything identical: the same robot arm, the same design and proportions, the same camera angle, the same position and size in the frame, the same lighting and the same flat pure green (#00FF00) background. The forearm and wrist stay exactly where they are.

Change only the pose of the hand: all five fingers open and gently spread, relaxed and slightly curved, as if about to take hold of something in front of it.
```

## Video prompts of the earlier attempt (Google Flow / Veo)

Settings: Frames to Video with `frame-1-start.png` as the first frame and `frame-2-end.png` as the last, Veo 3.1 Quality, 16:9, 8 seconds, 2–4 outputs, download with the 1080p upscale. Audio is not used.

```text
Locked-off studio product shot, camera completely static on a tripod, no camera movement at all.

A sleek white humanoid robot arm glides smoothly in from the left side of the frame toward a white fibre-optic Wi-Fi router that stands still on the right. The arm extends with calm, precise, confident mechanical motion. As it approaches, the fingers articulate slightly: the index finger straightens while the other fingers curl back a little. The index fingertip gently presses the tip of the nearest antenna and holds the touch. At the exact moment of contact, one very soft white glow pulses once at the fingertip and fades. The hand then stays perfectly still, touching the antenna, until the end of the clip.

The robot hand has glossy white ceramic shell panels over black graphite mechanical joints with polished chrome pins. The router is matte white with a row of slim white antennas and a circular perforated vent on its front; it never moves, bends or changes shape.

Seamless pure white studio backdrop and white floor that stay exactly the same in every frame. Soft diffused high-key lighting from above, gentle soft shadow under the arm that follows the arm as it moves. Strictly monochrome palette: white, light grey and black only.

Photorealistic, premium technology commercial, ultra sharp focus, clean edges, smooth natural robotic motion with ease-in and ease-out, 24 fps.

No camera movement, no zoom, no cuts, no text, no letters, no logos, no brand marks, no people, no extra objects, no colour accents, no lens flare, no flicker, no morphing, no warping of the router or antennas, no dust or particles.
```
